IN-VI Kids – seasonal website preview

Seasonal schedule:
- October 1–November 30: original website background + subtle falling autumn leaves.
- December 1–31: Christmas background + animated snowfall.
- January 1–February 28 (or 29 in leap years): original website background + animated snowfall.
- From March 1 through September 30: original website, no seasonal effects.

Local preview (add the parameter to index.html or adventures.html):
- ?autumn=1 shows subtle falling autumn leaves.
- ?christmas=1 shows the Christmas background and snowfall at any time.
- ?snow=1 shows snowfall only, over the original website design.

Autumn leaves are intentionally subtle: 12 on desktop and 7 on mobile. The existing logo, navigation, page content and Santa baked into the Christmas background are unchanged.
